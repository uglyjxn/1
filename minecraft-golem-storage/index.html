Copper Golem Storage Room

Copper Golem Storage Room

A 40 × 34 × 15 room, planned only for function. Five levels of 1-wide aisles with chests stacked three high on both sides, cut into sealed pods of nine sorting chests, each with its own golem and its own input chest. The layout follows what the golem can reach, and every number below is counted from the same model as the pictures.
