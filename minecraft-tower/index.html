Hall Floor Designs

Twenty floor designs for one big hall in Minecraft Java 26.2: ten decorative patterns, then ten realistic stone floors. Every design covers a 35 by 58 block area and comes with its block palette, a textured plan, an in-game view and a build guide.

The floor is read as 35 blocks wide (west to east) and 58 blocks long (north to south), 2,030 blocks in all. Only long-standing blocks are used, so none of it depends on anything specific to 26.2.
