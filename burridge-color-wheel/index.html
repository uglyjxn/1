HOW TO USE

  1. Dominant color (1): the overall color of the painting.
  2. Focal point color (2): the color opposite; use it sparingly, or gray it down.
  3. Spice colors (3, 4): the two side points; sprinkle them near the focal point.
  4. Black and white paint go along with these four colors.

Click a wedge, or drag the diamond, to point it at your dominant color. Shift-click a wedge to choose a color to mix with. Click a color row to copy its hex.
