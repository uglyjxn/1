Mix

Shift-click a wedge to choose the color to mix with the dominant color.

Summary

  1. The painting can have an overall Dominant color.
  2. Use the Focal Point color (opposite the dominant) sparingly, or gray the color down and use it in a larger area.
  3. Two Spice colors may be sprinkled sparingly near the focal point color.

Note: black and white paint go along with these four colors. Click or drag the diamond to point it at your dominant color. Mixes inside one of the four sections of the wheel stay bright and clean; cross the line and they dull down.
