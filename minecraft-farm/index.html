Mob Spawner Farm

Three spawner chambers feed one pen by water. You stand in a gallery 2.5 blocks back from the pen edge, where mobs can’t reach you but your sword can reach them, and kill them by hand. Hoppers move all the drops into one shared chest. Built for Minecraft Java 26.2 and sized for mobs from a zombie (1.95 tall) down to a cave spider (0.5 tall).

    Does every mob size work?

    Mob hitboxes in Java: zombie 0.6 wide and 1.95 tall, spider 1.4 wide and 0.9 tall, cave spider 0.7 wide and 0.5 tall. Every passage is checked against all three, and so is the one distance that matters most: how far you stand from the pen edge.
