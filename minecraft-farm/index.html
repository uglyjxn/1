Mob Spawner Farm

Four spawner chambers around one kill room. Water carries every mob to a pit under your feet, you kill them by hand through a hatch, and hoppers move the drops into a chest. Built for Minecraft Java 26.2 and sized for mobs from a zombie (1.95 tall) down to a cave spider (0.5 tall).

How it works

    3D view

    Use the height slider or a preset to peel the roofs off and look inside. The two cut buttons slice the farm in half.

    Does every mob size fit?

    Mob hitboxes in Java: zombie 0.6 wide and 1.95 tall, spider 1.4 wide and 0.9 tall, cave spider 0.7 wide and 0.5 tall. Every passage the mobs use is checked against all three.
